Bolna AI Customer Feedback Calling & Analysis

Automated post-purchase feedback calls that find unhappy customers, capture what went wrong and give store teams a tracked list of actions.

AI voice callingAutomation
1 · Business problem

Customers who had a bad purchase experience rarely told the business, so issues went unresolved and low ratings were found too late, if at all.

2 · Objective

Automate post-purchase feedback collection through AI voice calls, flag low ratings, and help stores take corrective action on customer issues.

3 · My role & approach
  • Designed the end-to-end flow from invoice to resolution
  • Passed customer and purchase details to the AI caller with Google Apps Script
  • Built analysis and follow-up tracking for store teams
